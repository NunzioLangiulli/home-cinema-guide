# Setting names by brand

> ⚠️ **Read first.** Menus change **every year** and between models of the same brand; translated menus vary further. This table is a starting point compiled from public guides and reviews, **not** a certified reference. For your exact model the most reliable source is the *"Calibration Settings"* page on [RTINGS](https://www.rtings.com/tv/learn/how-to-calibrate-your-tv) (e.g. `rtings.com/tv/reviews/<brand>/<model>/settings`) or the manual.
>
> **The most useful contribution to this project:** correcting or completing this table with brand, **model and year**, firmware and a photo of the menu. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Reliability label for the whole table: ❓ to be verified row by row.

## Privacy (content recognition, ACR)

| Brand | Where to look | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Samsung | Settings → Support/General → Terms & Privacy → **Viewing Information Services** and interest-based advertising | [Malwarebytes, March 2026](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/03/samsung-tvs-stop-spying-on-viewers-in-texas-heres-how-to-disable-acr-anywhere) |
| LG | Settings → General → System → Additional settings/User agreements → **Live Plus** and "viewing information" | V5; [Fox 7, Texas–LG settlement](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/lg-smart-tv-acr-data-collection-texas-settlement) |
| Sony | Settings → Privacy / **Samba Interactive TV** (Samba TV's ACR); Google ad settings | V5 |
| Google TV (Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips…) | Settings → Privacy → Usage & diagnostics; Ads → turn off personalization | — |
| Roku TV | Settings → Privacy → Smart TV Experience → **Use info from TV inputs** | — |
| Vizio | Settings → System → Reset & Admin → **Viewing Data** | [ProPublica 2015](https://www.propublica.org/article/own-a-vizio-smart-tv-its-watching-you) |

Paths change with updates. The most current, community-maintained per-brand steps are in [CleanYourTV](https://cleanyour.tv/not-android). If you can't find the item, search for "ACR", "Viewing information", "Live Plus", "Samba" or "info from TV inputs".
